Princeton meminta dua surat dari guru di **bidang akademik yang berbeda**. Surat Bapak

tidak perlu menyentuh kemampuan bahasa atau karakter umum. Yang dicari dari Bapak adalah

siapa pun dari transkrip.**

Common App meminta satu lampiran tambahan yang mudah terlewat: **daftar mata pelajaran
yang Bapak ajarkan ke Tristan, beserta tingkat kesulitannya.** Cukup satu paragraf.

## Draf surat

To the Admissions Committee,

I am the Head of the Software and Game Development programme at SMK TI Bali Global
Badung and I hold a master's degree in computer science. I have taught Tristan in our
vocational computing subjects and supervised him as a competition mentor. There are 214
students in his year group. I will try to describe him precisely rather than warmly.

The first thing I noticed about Tristan was not talent. It was that he asked why the
syllabus was the way it was.

When we began teaching server-side programming, our curriculum uses PHP. Tristan asked
why. He wanted to know why we were not teaching JavaScript, which he argued was faster
and more current, and whether MongoDB would not suit the projects better than the
relational database we use. This is not a question our students ask. Most of them are
learning their first language and have no basis to compare.

I told him to find out, and he did. What he came back with was better than the question.
He had worked out that the choice is not about which technology is more modern, but about
what the application is actually doing, and that database efficiency is a property of the
workload rather than of the tool. He arrived at a more sophisticated answer than the one
I would have given him.

That pattern has repeated for three years. When we teach a design principle, he tests it
against a case where it fails. When the class learns basic database operations, he is
already working on something several levels above the lesson. While his classmates were
learning to create, read, update and delete records, he was implementing deep learning
models he had taught himself through external programmes.

I have said, more than once, that his project work is at the standard of an undergraduate
final thesis, and in some cases a master's thesis. I want to be careful about that claim,
because it is easy to say and hard to justify. What I mean specifically is: the projects
have a stated problem, a method chosen for reasons he can defend, an implementation he
built rather than assembled, and an honest account of what did not work. That last
element is the rarest. Most students at this level present only what succeeded. (berlebihan ga sih?)

[GAP: Pak Ade — nama proyek spesifik yang Bapak nilai, dan apa yang membuatnya setara
skripsi. Satu proyek yang Bapak ingat detailnya lebih berharga daripada penilaian umum. (breeva itu sendiri)]

I should tell the committee about an incident that reflects on me as much as on him.

When entering marks for the programming module, I made an error and recorded the same
mark, 88, for the whole class. Tristan came to ask about his. He did not complain and he
did not assume. He asked whether the mark was right, because he thought it did not match
the work he had submitted. It was not right, and I corrected it. (wait, kayanya ini ga bagus kalau ditulis secara eksplisit kalau pak ade salah, atau gimana?)

What has stayed with me is not that he checked. It is what he said afterwards: that he
did not know whether the other students' marks were also wrong. He was not certain his
classmates had noticed, and he did not think they would ask.

He is a student who audits the person grading him, and then worries about the people who
did not.

The same disposition appears in how he treats what he learns. He coached a classmate
through a web design competition that the classmate went on to win. As class
representative, he rebuilt the way our students approached study targets, and the class
average improved. He does not hold on to what he works out. (emang bagus mention hal yang sdah di mention guru bk sebelumnya?)

I know that his interests have moved past what I teach. He has been working independently
on mathematics that is well outside our curriculum and outside my field, and he has been
corresponding with a university researcher about it. I cannot assess that work and I will
not pretend to. What I can say is that it is the same behaviour I have watched for three
years, applied to a harder object: he found a question, discovered no one had answered it,
and went to find someone who could tell him whether the question was real.

Of the 214 students in this year group, I would place him first without hesitation. I
would place him first among every student I have taught in this programme. He is the
only one who has ever corrected me.

[GAP: nama, jabatan, tanda tangan, tanggal]

## Catatan untuk Tristan

Cerita nilai 88 itu bagian terkuat dari surat ini, dan risikonya nol untukmu — yang
mengaku salah adalah gurunya, bukan kamu. Tapi hanya Pak Ade yang berhak menceritakannya.
Kalau dia tidak nyaman, hormati itu dan minta dia ganti dengan momen lain.

Yang masih kosong dan hanya kamu bisa isi: **nama proyek spesifik** yang membuat Pak Ade
mengatakan "setara skripsi". Tanpa itu, kalimat paling kuat di surat ini berdiri tanpa bukti.

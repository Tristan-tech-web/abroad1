### Reporting period
Marks are reported by semester. The three-year programme comprises six semesters,
Grade 10 through Grade 12. Each subject receives a single numerical mark per semester.

### Scale
All subjects are marked on a **0–100** scale.

**Minimum passing mark (KKM): [GAP: 75?]**
A mark below the KKM requires remediation before credit is awarded.

**Note on distribution.** In practice the working range of marks reported at this school
is approximately [GAP: 85]–[GAP: 98]. The lower half of the nominal 0–100 scale is not in
routine use. Reading these marks as US percentages will understate student performance.

### No weighting, no advanced tiers
The Indonesian SMK curriculum is fixed. There are no honours, Advanced Placement,
International Baccalaureate, or Cambridge courses available in this system, so no
weighting is applied and no distinction between course difficulty levels appears on
this transcript. All students in a programme take the same subjects in the same sequence.

### Subject groups

**National core (all students, all programmes)**
Religious Education · Pancasila and Civics · Indonesian Language · English ·
Mathematics · Natural and Social Sciences (IPAS) · History · Physical Education ·
Arts and Culture · Entrepreneurship (PKWU) · Balinese Language

**Vocational subjects (programme-specific)**
Marked on the transcript as *Produktif*. For PPLG (Software and Game Development) these
cover programming, web and mobile development, databases, software engineering,
game development, and introductory artificial intelligence and machine learning.

### Two items with no US equivalent

**PKL — Praktik Kerja Lapangan (Industrial Placement)**
A compulsory placement of up to six months during Grade 12, served at a partner company
and assessed jointly by the employer and the school. Students are assigned production
work, not observation. This appears on the transcript as a single assessed component.

**UKK — Uji Kompetensi Keahlian (Competency Examination)**
A terminal practical examination assessed against Indonesian national occupational
standards, in part by external industry assessors from outside the school.

### Class rank
The school designates an annual overall academic ranking, *Juara Umum*.

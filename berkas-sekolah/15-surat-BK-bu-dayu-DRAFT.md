Ida Ayu Cempaka Putri, S.Pd. — Waka Kesiswaan dan Guru BK

## Untuk Bu Dayu, sebelum membaca

Surat ini draf, bukan naskah untuk ditandatangani. Mohon ditulis ulang dengan kalimat
Ibu sendiri. Alasannya ada di berkas 10, dan singkatnya: tiga surat yang berirama sama
dari tiga guru berbeda akan langsung terbaca sebagai satu tangan oleh panitia penerimaan,
dan itu merugikan Tristan.

Batas maksimal **1000 kata**. Draf ini sekitar 860 kata, jadi masih ada ruang.

Surat konselor berbeda dari surat guru mata pelajaran. Panitia penerimaan tidak menanyakan
nilai kepada Ibu, karena nilai sudah ada di transkrip. Yang mereka minta dari Ibu adalah
**konteks**: sekolah ini seperti apa, posisi Tristan di dalamnya, dan keadaan di luar
sekolah yang memengaruhi apa yang bisa dan tidak bisa dia lakukan.

Formulirnya secara khusus meminta konselor membahas situasi keluarga atau kewajiban di
luar sekolah, baik yang positif maupun yang negatif. Itu bukan pertanyaan basa-basi.
Itu bagian yang paling sering menentukan.

## Draf surat

To the Admissions Committee,

I have known Tristan for three years. I am the Head of Student Affairs at SMK TI Bali
Global Badung and one of two counsellors serving a school of [GAP] students, with a
graduating class of 214.

I want to begin with the way I know him, because it is not the usual way a counsellor
comes to know a student. For two years Tristan has come to my office before every
competition he has entered. Not afterwards, to report a result. Beforehand, to think
aloud about whether the thing was worth entering at all, and what he would learn if he
lost it. I have had many students tell me what they won. He is the only one who has
consistently told me what he was uncertain about first.

Some context about this school may be useful. We are a vocational secondary school.
In the Indonesian system, SMK is the track designed for direct entry into employment,
not for university. Our curriculum is fixed by national regulation; students do not
choose their courses, and there is no honours, Advanced Placement, or International
Baccalaureate programme available anywhere in this track. Our institutional goal is that
90 per cent of graduates enter work, further study, or their own business. That is the
system operating as intended.

No student from this school has applied to a university outside Indonesia before.
Tristan is the first. I say this so the committee understands that when he pursued
something beyond our curriculum, there was no established path for him to follow and no
one here who had walked it.

What he did instead was enter competitions that were not meant for him. He won first
place in a web development competition open to university students, where he was the
only secondary school student to place. He took second at a design competition hosted by
Politeknik Negeri Bali, again the only secondary student among university entrants. Most
significantly, he placed third in the Microsoft AI Impact Challenge, a national
competition open to working professionals and university teams, with more than three
hundred entrants and three stages of selection. He was the only vocational secondary
school student to reach the final three. [GAP: Ibu mungkin ingat detail yang saya tidak
tahu — persiapannya berapa lama, apa yang dia bawa ke Ibu sebelum lomba itu.]

He also holds a bronze medal in the national mathematics olympiad for vocational schools,
and he ranks second overall in his year group [GAP: mohon pastikan cakupannya —
dari 214 siswa seangkatan, atau dari kelasnya].

The committee should know the circumstances in which this was done. Tristan's father
sells rice meals from a small warung at the front of the family house. His mother manages
the household. Neither parent completed a university degree [GAP: mohon konfirmasi status
pendidikan ibunya sebelum menulis kalimat ini]. Tristan has shared domestic
responsibilities throughout his time here. This school has waived or reduced his tuition
on several occasions in recognition of his competition results, which I mention because
it is the plainest indicator of both his standing here and his family's circumstances.

I was asked whether his academic record accurately reflects his ability. I do not think
it does, and I want to be specific rather than flattering about why. Our marks are
compressed: the working range at this school is roughly 85 to 98, and the difference
between a very good student and an exceptional one is a few points that a foreign reader
cannot interpret. Our curriculum also has no ceiling for a student to press against.
There is no advanced mathematics here, no research course, no independent study. The
work that would actually demonstrate what Tristan can do sits entirely outside our
timetable, where we cannot grade it.

I should also tell the committee something less comfortable, because I would rather they
hear it from me. Tristan does not follow rules he considers unjustified, and he will say
so. He argues about the dress code. He arrives seconds before the bell rather than
minutes. He questions requirements that are stated without reasons. [GAP: Bu Dayu — mohon
tulis ulang bagian ini dengan penilaian Ibu sendiri, dan tolong cek apakah ada catatan
disiplin tertulis di berkas sekolah. Kalau tidak ada, sebutkan itu. Kalau ada, lebih baik
disebut di sini daripada ditemukan.]

I will say what I have observed. The pattern is consistent: he does not break rules that
have a stated reason, and he does not break them quietly or dishonestly. He argues,
openly, and accepts the answer when there is one. As a counsellor responsible for
discipline in this school, I have found him easier to work with than students who comply
without understanding, because I always know where he stands.

When he served as class representative, he replaced our usual approach of uniform study
discipline with a system in which students set their own targets and were left alone once
they met them. The class average rose. He had worked out for himself, after failing to
keep to a rigid schedule of his own, that the rigidity was the problem rather than the
effort. He then gave that conclusion away to thirty other people.

I recommend him without reservation, and I would like the committee to know that this
recommendation is the first of its kind I have written.

[GAP: nama, jabatan, tanda tangan, tanggal]

## Catatan untuk Tristan

Paragraf aturan itu risiko yang saya ambil sengaja. Panitia penerimaan di kampus seperti
Pitzer, Grinnell, dan Oberlin merespons baik konselor yang berterus terang, dan paragraf
itu mengubah sesuatu yang bisa muncul sebagai kejutan menjadi karakter yang dijelaskan.

Tapi ini keputusan Bu Dayu dan keputusanmu. Kalau ada catatan disiplin tertulis di
berkas sekolah, paragraf ini wajib ada. Kalau tidak ada sama sekali, boleh dipotong —
tapi tanyakan dulu, jangan berasumsi.
